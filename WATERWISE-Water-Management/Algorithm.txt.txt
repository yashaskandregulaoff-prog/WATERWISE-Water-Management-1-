1. Start the program.
2. Declare a 2D array usage[5][7] and a 1D array dailyTarget[5].
3. Input daily water consumption for all 5 zones and their daily targets.
4. Validate all inputs and re-enter invalid values.
5. Display the complete water consumption table.
6. For each zone:
   - Calculate weekly total and daily average.
   - Find the highest consumption and its day.
   - Count over-limit days and calculate total excess water.
   - Detect sudden spikes greater than 140% of the previous day.
   - Calculate unique alert days.
7. Generate and display the water audit report.
8. Rank all five zones based on total excess water, giving equal ranks for ties.
9. Display conservation suggestions for each zone.
10. Display the menu:
    - 1 – Display Water Data
    - 2 – Generate Audit Report
    - 3 – Rank Zones
    - 4 – Conservation Actions
    - 5 – Exit
11. Allow options 3 and 4 only after generating the audit report.
12. Repeat the menu until the user selects Exit.
13. Stop the program.
This algorithm is short, easy to write in your record, and covers the main requirements of your WATERWISE assignment.
